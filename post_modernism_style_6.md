# Postmodernism Style 6: City of Arts and Sciences by Santiago Calatrava

## The Building

The City of Arts and Sciences in Valencia, Spain, designed by Santiago Calatrava and completed in the early 2000s, is a powerful example of postmodern architecture shaped by spectacle, movement, and expressive structural innovation. The complex is not a single building but a collection of sculptural forms linked by a strong cultural narrative.

Its white, skeletal structures, sweeping curves, and dramatic bridges give the whole ensemble the feel of an urban sculpture park. The architecture seems to echo the language of living organisms, wings, and motion, creating an image that is both futuristic and deeply theatrical.

## What Makes Its Style Distinctive

- **Sculptural, organism-like forms:** The buildings are shaped like abstract living forms, emphasizing flexibility and motion rather than rigid geometry.
- **Expressive engineering:** Structural systems are often visible and celebrated, turning the building into a demonstration of architectural logic and artistry.
- **White surfaces and dramatic light:** The light color palette heightens the sense of sleekness and amplifies the sculptural silhouettes.
- **Urban spectacle:** The project is designed to act as a major public image, not simply as a set of functional spaces.
- **A mixture of science, culture, and spectacle:** The complex blends research, leisure, and civic identity into an unmistakably expressive architectural statement.
- **Postmodern energy:** It embraces bold visual drama, symbolism, and a sense of invention that goes beyond pure efficiency.

## Why It Matters

The City of Arts and Sciences is important because it pushes architecture beyond the restrictions of conventional utilitarian design. It presents culture and technology as a unified experience through highly expressive form. The buildings feel like engineered sculptures, making the entire site into a public landmark.

This approach reflects a distinctly postmodern attitude: architecture can be symbolic, theatrical, and intellectually ambitious while still being practical. The result is a project that is both programmatically functional and visually unforgettable.

## Style at a Glance

| Feature | How it appears in the complex |
|---|---|
| Form | Dynamic, sculptural, and fluid |
| Structure | Expressed and visible rather than hidden |
| Material palette | White surfaces emphasize clarity and light |
| Urban role | Acts as a civic landmark and cultural symbol |
| Postmodern character | Performance, spectacle, and expressive engineering dominate the design |
