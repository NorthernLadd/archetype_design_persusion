# Victoria Pereyra - Brand Archetype

## My Brand Archetype

My primary brand archetype is the **Jester**. The Jester is connected to fun, humor, spontaneity, and making everyday experiences more enjoyable. This archetype fits someone who values laughter, enjoys being around people, and does not want life to become too boring or repetitive.

My secondary archetypes are the **Lover** and the **Hero**. The Lover adds a focus on meaningful relationships, caring about people, and creating positive experiences with the people who matter to me. The Hero adds the goal of accomplishing something and continuing to move forward when things do not go as planned.

## Why AI Chose the Jester

AI identified the Jester as my main archetype because many of my answers focused on fun, people, and enjoying life. I said that I enjoy hanging out and having fun with people, admire funny and charismatic people, and would naturally choose a concert or party. I also said that being described as fun to be around is an important compliment to me.

Other answers supported this result too. I am drawn to a playful or chaotic vibe, value spontaneous experiences, and would want to create an enjoyable experience if my friends were bored. When something does not go my way, I tend to move on and have fun instead of dwelling on it. I would also be bothered by life becoming boring and repetitive, and I would be happiest looking back on many amazing and hilarious experiences.

Together, these answers show the Jester's interest in energy, laughter, social connection, and making ordinary moments more memorable.

## Secondary Archetypes

The **Lover** appeared as a secondary archetype because I value meaningful experiences with people I care about. When someone I care about is having a bad day, my instinct is to listen and make sure they know they are not alone. This shows that fun is not the only thing that matters to me; I also care about connection, support, and being present for other people.

The **Hero** appeared as a third archetype because I want to accomplish something. Even though I prefer to move on and keep a positive attitude when things go wrong, that does not mean I give up. The Hero side of my archetype reflects the motivation to achieve goals and keep going through challenges.
